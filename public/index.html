Where it began

Dina's Studio started small — a handful of matching sets sewn between late nights, built on the idea that modest fashion shouldn't have to choose between comfort and elegance. What began as pieces made for friends and family quickly grew into a studio of its own, built one abaya, one kimono, and one heart-stitched detail at a time.

"I wanted every piece to feel like it was made for someone I love — because it was."
D
Dina
Founder & Designer

The inspiration

Every collection draws from soft, romantic fabric hues and flowing silhouettes, all while showcasing the expertise of our tailors through every piece. The heart in our logo isn't just a mark, it's a promise: that every stitch is made with the same care we'd want for our own family.
